OUR PHILOSOPHY
What we do

Two strands, one evidence base

Q²ERA is named for the integration of Qualitative inquiry and Quantitative analysis. We support researchers across the full journey — from refining a question to communicating the finding.

Qualitative

Qualitative Research

  • Study design and methodology
  • In-depth interview and focus group analysis
  • Thematic, framework, and content analysis
  • Codebook development and systematic coding
  • Reflexive thematic analysis
  • Software support (NVivo, MAXQDA, ATLAS.ti, QualCoder)
  • Interpretation and theme development
Quantitative

Quantitative Research

  • Study design and methodology
  • Data management and cleaning
  • Descriptive and inferential statistical analysis
  • Survey data analysis and interpretation
  • Statistical modelling and regression analysis
  • Data visualisation and reporting
Translation

Evidence Translation

  • Mixed-methods research integration
  • Interpretation and synthesis of findings
  • Manuscript development and scientific writing
  • Results presentation, tables, and figures
  • Reporting guideline alignment
  • Publication preparation and revision support
How we work

The research journey, start to finish

Whether you need one step or the whole journey, we meet you where your study currently stands.

01

02

Design the study

Choose the methodology and instruments that fit.

03

Analyse the data

Apply rigorous qualitative and/or quantitative analysis.

04

Interpret findings

Make sense of results in context, without overreach.

05

Communicate evidence

Write up and prepare for publication or presentation.
